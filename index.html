02 PERSONAL NOTE

This Is Me...

I carry the smell of rain on soil, the sound of Kalhor’s kamancheh, bitter coffee and a blue BIC pen. My mother’s touch. My father’s laugh. Friends who feel like home. Cinema stays with me: Alfredo’s last gift, John Coffey’s tears, John Keating’s faith in a voice. I return to poetry and the moments that catch in the throat. Even as they happen, I know I will miss them.

That is what I keep…

That is where my stories begin…

POV & Manifesto · 03

I design stories people can enter.

I see every brand as a world in the making. My work is to find the human truth, define the creative territory, build the story system, and translate it into experiences people can feel, remember, and take part in. Concept design and gamification turn brand narratives into living spaces that audiences step into willingly.

01 / Foundation

Human Insight

Uncovering the quiet anxieties, unexpressed desires, and deep-seated motivations that drive genuine human behavior.

02 / Tension

Cultural Tension

Pinpointing the friction between traditional habits, generational change, and daily rituals that creates societal attention.

03 / Habitat

Creative Territory

Carving out an imaginative, distinctive, and credible domain that the brand can authoritatively own with its unique voice.

04 / Ecosystem

Story System

Architecting touchpoints, meaningful ceremonies, and gamified mechanics that inspire people to stay, return, and participate.
