HOW I THINK · 03

First frame the right problem. Then build the marketing system around it.

Marketing is not a collection of channels. It is a decision system connecting the business problem, human behavior, brand position and experience. Creativity is not the final layer; used well, it gives the whole system energy.

01 / PROBLEM

Business Problem

What actually needs to change: growth, sales, adoption, retention, position or experience?

02 / HUMAN

Human Insight

What do people want, fear and do — especially when behavior and stated preference diverge?

03 / CHOICE

Market & Brand Strategy

Where do we play, for whom, with what promise and with what priorities?

04 / SYSTEM

Experience & Creative System

How should product, message, content, CX and creativity work in one direction?

Business Problem → Human Insight → Marketing & Brand Strategy → Experience & Creativity

02 PERSONAL NOTE

This Is Me...

I love the smell of rain on soil, Kalhor’s kamancheh, a blue BIC pen, the dry bitterness of coffee, my mother’s touch and my father’s laugh. I love my friends and the friendships that feel like home. I love good films — Alfredo and the final scene of Cinema Paradiso, John Coffey’s tears in The Green Mile, John Keating’s words in Dead Poets Society.

I love the poems of Ebtehaj, Akhavan and Moshiri, and those rare moments when happiness almost hurts — when you know, while the moment is still happening, that it is already becoming a memory you will miss.

Life, for me, is made of those moments — those loves.
